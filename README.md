#  AI Summarizer (Gemini API)

Tired of reading long articles or research papers? This project helps you quickly turn them into **easy-to-read summaries**.  
Built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, and powered by **Google’s Gemini API**, it takes any text you give it and condenses it into **clear, concise bullet points**.  

Whether you’re a student, researcher, or just someone who wants the gist of an article fast — this tool saves time and effort. 

---

##  What It Does
- Summarizes articles, essays, or any long text into **short notes**.  
- Provides **quick, clear bullet points** so you don’t miss key ideas.  
- Comes with a **clean dark-themed UI** for a distraction-free experience.  
- Works in real-time — paste, click, and get your summary instantly!  

---

## How to Use
1. Paste your text into the box.  
2. Hit the **Summarize** button.  
3. Get your summary in seconds.

---

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript  
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API  

---
